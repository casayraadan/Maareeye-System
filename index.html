About MAAREEYE

Building the most capable, friendly, and fast AI toolkit for everyday professionals. No clutter — just tools that work.

Our Mission

MAAREEYE was born out of frustration with clunky, ad-filled file utilities online. We believe basic digital tasks — converting a document, extracting text, or removing a background — should be seamless and joyful.

By leveraging modern AI models via lightning-fast edge networks, we give you enterprise-grade capabilities wrapped in an approachable design.

The Tools

Vision AI
